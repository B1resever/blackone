Delete your ONE account

If account creation is enabled in ONE, you can request deletion from the account settings inside the app. You may also request deletion through this external process.

External deletion request

Contact BLACK ONE support from the email address associated with your ONE account and include the subject ONE Account Deletion Request. Include only the information necessary to identify the account. Additional identity verification may be required to protect your account.

What is deleted

ONE will delete the account and personal data associated with it that is not required to be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, payment, safety, tax, or transportation-recordkeeping obligations.

Timing

Requests are processed as soon as reasonably practicable. If certain records must be retained, the user will be informed through the support process.
