# B1 LAB — Brand system v0.1 (PROPOSED; approval pending)

Scope: B1 LAB Medical Services Network only. Do not apply to B1 passenger transportation, NODO, or other brands.

## Positioning
B1 LAB is a proposed 24/7 service-request and professional-matching technology platform for mobile phlebotomy and other properly licensed or qualified services. Availability 24/7 is a platform design objective; do not claim a professional is available 24/7 in a geographic area until coverage is verified. Do not imply B1 LAB is a medical provider, emergency service, licensed agency, or HIPAA-certified organization without legal/operational verification.

## Brand architecture
- Master: B1 LAB
- Descriptor (proposed): Medical Services Network
- Products: B1 LAB Business, B1 LAB Professional, B1 LAB Command Center
- Tagline (proposed, not approved): Trusted Professionals. Seamless Care.
- Languages: EN and ES. Preserve B1 LAB name and visual consistency in both.

## Visual tokens (from first concept, NOT FINAL)
- Primary navy: #103A53 — professional trust, high legibility on light surfaces
- Accent teal: #19B8A6 — action/status highlights, never sole indicator of meaning
- White: #FFFFFF — default background
- Typography proposal: Montserrat, licensed appropriately; system font fallback
- Icon concept: B1 lettering with droplet and location marker; concept art only, NOT production vector or cleared trademark
- No black-background-first design. Prefer generous whitespace, clean hierarchy, restrained icons, accessible contrast.

## Image and photography policy
- Use real, properly licensed photographs; no stock imagery presented as actual B1 LAB staff, patients, locations, equipment or completed services.
- Capture explicit model/property releases and patient consent when required.
- Never display patient names, test results, labels, charts, addresses, or identifiable protected health information in marketing mockups.
- Medical procedures depicted must use correct PPE, equipment, scope of practice, and realistic safe technique; review by qualified clinician.
- Alt text in EN/ES, high-resolution crops, consistent color treatment, mobile-first layout.
- Record provenance, rights, subject release, geographic context and approval owner for each asset.

## UI governance
- Shared design tokens for website, mobile apps, email, PDF, provider onboarding and command center.
- Role-specific labels and screens; do not confuse Business customers with Professionals.
- States: requested, credentials_pending, available, offered, accepted, en_route, arrived, service_in_progress, completed, canceled, incident.
- Status color must always have readable text; WCAG AA contrast and keyboard/screen-reader testing.
- Payments and credential verification must use real backend states; no hardcoded green success.
- Do not promise clinical availability, verified credentials, insurance or licensing before source-backed verification.

## Professional onboarding (requirements proposal)
Identity; license and primary-source check where applicable; certification/training; professional liability coverage; allowed procedures; expiration tracking; background checks where legally required; independent contractor terms; applicable privacy obligations; local jurisdiction and service radius. Massage therapists are a proposed separate category requiring applicable Florida licensure and legal review.

## Required legal gate
Independent contractor labeling and waivers do not remove statutory, privacy, patient-safety, employment, agency or marketplace duties. Florida healthcare marketplace/nurse registry/home health agency and massage establishment requirements must be assessed by qualified counsel before enabling categories. No clinical services should be represented as live before this review.

## QA release gates
1. Brand owner approves logo, names, colors, typography and copy.
2. Final vector SVG, PNG, app icons and favicon generated from approved master.
3. Trademark and domain clearance reviewed.
4. Asset licenses and releases logged.
5. WCAG accessibility and EN/ES copy QA.
6. Clinical claims reviewed; privacy/legal review completed.
7. App screens and end-to-end flows tested with synthetic data.
8. Production release approved only after infrastructure, billing and security verification.
