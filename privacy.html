ONE Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026

ONE is a transportation platform operated by Open Skies Holdings LLC under the BLACK ONE brand. This policy explains how information is handled when you use ONE, request a ride, communicate with support, or use driver services.

Information we collect

Depending on the feature used, ONE may collect identity and contact information, reservation details, pickup and destination information, trip status, device and app diagnostics, driver and vehicle verification information, and payment-related transaction identifiers. Card details are processed by payment providers such as Stripe and are not intended to be stored directly by ONE.

Location

Location may be used for pickup, routing, trip progress, driver arrival, safety, and operational support. Where the app offers location access, users should also be able to enter an address manually when location access is not granted.

How information is used

Information is used to provide reservations and transportation services, calculate and confirm pricing, process payments, communicate about a trip, support safety and fraud prevention, comply with legal obligations, and improve reliability.

Service providers

ONE may use service providers for hosting, databases, maps and routing, payment processing, communications, analytics, and application distribution. These providers receive only the information necessary for the services they provide and are expected to protect it appropriately.

Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, meet financial and transportation recordkeeping obligations, and comply with applicable law. Some records may need to be retained after an account deletion request when legally required.

Your choices and deletion

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information. If ONE account creation is enabled, users can initiate account deletion within the app and through the account-deletion page on the BLACK ONE website.

Security

ONE uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information in transit and at rest. No system can guarantee absolute security.

International use

The ONE app may be available for download internationally while transportation services are enabled only in selected operating markets. Information may be processed in the United States or other locations where service providers operate, subject to applicable law.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to BLACK ONE support through the official website and application support channels.

This policy will be updated as ONE adds accounts, live GPS tracking, driver verification, communications, or other production features.
