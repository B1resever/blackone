ONE Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026

ONE is a transportation platform operated by Open Skies Holdings LLC under the BLACK ONE brand. This policy explains how information is handled when you use ONE, request a ride, communicate with support, or use driver services.

Information we collect

Depending on the feature used, ONE may collect identity and contact information, account/session identifiers, reservation details, pickup and destination information, trip status and operational events, driver and vehicle verification information, notification/device identifiers, trip communications, and payment-related transaction identifiers. Card details are processed by payment providers such as Stripe and are not intended to be stored directly by ONE.

Location

Pickup and destination addresses are used for routing and reservations. During an active assigned trip, an authenticated driver may explicitly enable foreground live-location sharing. While that feature is active, ONE may process the driver's latitude, longitude, location accuracy, heading, speed, timestamp, and the reservation/driver identifiers needed to limit access to the assigned trip.

Driver live location is used for pickup coordination, passenger trip visibility, safety, and operational support. Stage 1 does not request background location permission and does not continuously collect passenger GPS. Manual address entry remains available for reservation details.

Trip communications and ride code

Authenticated passengers and assigned drivers may exchange in-app trip messages. ONE also generates a ride code for a reservation so the assigned driver can confirm the passenger at pickup before the trip moves to the onboard state. Message content, ride-code verification state, and related trip events may be retained for service, safety, dispute-resolution, and fraud-prevention purposes.

How information is used

Information is used to provide reservations and transportation services, calculate and confirm pricing, process payments, communicate about a trip, assign compatible verified drivers and vehicles, provide live trip tools, support safety and fraud prevention, comply with legal obligations, and improve reliability.

Service providers

ONE may use service providers for hosting, databases, maps and routing, payment processing, push notifications and communications, and application distribution. These providers receive information needed for the services they provide and are expected to protect it appropriately.

Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, meet financial and transportation recordkeeping obligations, address safety matters, and comply with applicable law. Some records may need to be retained after an account deletion request when legally required.

Your choices and deletion

Users can choose whether to enable notifications. Driver foreground live-location sharing starts only when the driver activates the live-trip feature and can be stopped from ONE Driver. Users may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information. ONE accounts can initiate account deletion inside the app and through the account-deletion page on the BLACK ONE website.

Security

ONE uses administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information in transit and at rest, including authenticated trip access for passenger/driver communications and live-trip data. No system can guarantee absolute security.

International use

The ONE app may be available for download internationally while transportation services are enabled only in selected operating markets. Information may be processed in the United States or other locations where service providers operate, subject to applicable law.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to BLACK ONE support at reservations@blackonetransportation.com or through the official ONE support page.
